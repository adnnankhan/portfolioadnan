What I believe

Designing the future
Human × AI.

01Intent

Great products understand intent, not commands.

Technology should adapt to how people think, not force people to learn how technology works.

02Emotion

Emotion sits at the center of every experience.

Research, IA, UI and interactions only matter when they create meaningful human experiences.

03AI

AI should amplify human capability.

The best AI products don't replace people. They help people do more, decide better and move faster.

04Clarity

Good design creates clarity in uncertainty.

When technology evolves and requirements are unclear, design provides direction and confidence.
